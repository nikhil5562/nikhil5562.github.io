IMD Weather Assistant

Ask plain-language questions over the India Meteorological Department's daily Kerala weather reports.

  • Retrieval-augmented generation: report PDFs are chunked, embedded with bge-small into ChromaDB, and answered by Gemini with date filtering and cited sources.

Susthira — Kozhikode Climate Projection Dashboard

Local-body climate projections for Kozhikode District, 2000–2099, in the browser.

  • Covers all 78 local bodies — 70 grama panchayats, 7 municipalities and 1 corporation — from the CORDEX South Asia regional climate model ensemble.
  • Switch between RCP 2.6 and RCP 8.5 and scrub through the century: rainfall, temperature anomaly, diurnal range and a comfort index.
  • Area-weighted interpolation from model grid cells onto local-body boundaries, a Leaflet choropleth map and a rainwater-harvesting calculator.

2026Research

3-D volumetric radar from ISRO's TERLS Doppler radar

Rebuilds ISRO's 3-D gridded reflectivity product from raw polar Doppler Weather Radar scans.

  • Converts MOSDAC Level-2B polar volumes into 81 × 481 × 481 Cartesian cubes (1 km × 1 km × 250 m) with dual-polarisation clutter filtering and velocity dealiasing.
  • Checked against ISRO's official L2C product on four scan pairs: recovers 98.7% of official echo cells (correlation 0.71 where both have data), with the remaining coverage differences documented openly.
  • Detects and corrects silent quirks in the official files at load time — swapped lat/lon labels, zeroed sweep indices, bogus fill values — backed by unit tests and GitHub Actions CI.

2025–26In progress

3-D ConvLSTM radar nowcasting

Deep learning that forecasts how storm echoes evolve in three dimensions, up to two hours ahead.

  • Reads ten radar volumes at a 15-minute cadence and predicts the next eight — lead times T+15 to T+120 min.
  • Adapted the published 3D-ConvLSTM model (Sun et al., 2022) to Kochi radar — built the preprocessing pipeline and migrated it from TensorFlow 1.14 to 2.x — then wrote my own TERLS implementation with a coverage-aware input channel.
  • Training and held-out evaluation are under way.
  • TensorFlow
  • ConvLSTM
  • Python
  • NumPy
